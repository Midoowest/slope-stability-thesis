The core loop

Execution, review, evidence

Every step mirrors a real junior role. You receive work, you ship it, it gets judged against professional standards.

01

Accept the brief

A client posts context, a problem, objectives, and a deadline. You commit, then execute in the Mission War Room.

02

Submit the deliverable

Your AI Senior Manager guides your thinking — never hands you the answer. The work must be yours.

03

Pass the review

Approved, Exceptional, or Needs Revision. Approvals build reputation, raise your rank, and create portfolio assets.

Why it's different

Reputation over gamification

No XP, no confetti, no toy rewards. A transparent 0–100 credibility score you'd be proud to show an employer.

Professional identity

You execute missions and earn rank promotions — you don't take lessons or level up.

Evidence-first

Every approved mission becomes a shareable portfolio asset. The portfolio is the credential.

Rigorous review

A standards-driven mentor, not a cheerleader. Honest feedback: strengths, gaps, real-world impact, next step.

Earned progression

Associate → Specialist → Senior Specialist → Lead → Partner. Slow to build, meaningful to hold.

Context

Nimbus is launching a cold brew line for summer. Their audience is mobile-first, ages 22–35, and discovers products through social. They've validated demand — now they need pre-orders, not just awareness.

The problem

Their current page reads like a brochure. The pre-order action is buried below three scrolls of brand story, so interested visitors bounce before they ever reach it.

Objectives

  • Surface the pre-order action immediately, above the fold
  • Communicate the core product benefit in one line
  • Earn trust with a single, credible social-proof element
  • Stay fast and legible on a 360px phone

Deliverables

Responsive landing pageDesktop + mobile variant · figma, link, or file
One-line rationaleWhy your hero leads where it does

Acceptance criteria

  • Pre-order CTA visible above the fold
  • Responsive down to 360px width
  • Clear product benefit in the hero
  • One credible social-proof element
